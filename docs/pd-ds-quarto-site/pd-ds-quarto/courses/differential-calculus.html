Differential Calculus

Residuals, loss functions, derivatives, and optimization

Activities

Activity 1: Why residuals need a loss function

Students fit a line, compute residuals, and observe that positive and negative errors can cancel. They compare absolute error and squared error.

Activity 2: Smooth versus nonsmooth loss

Students graph absolute value loss and squared loss. They discuss why differentiability matters for optimization algorithms.

Activity 3: Find the best slope

Students treat slope as a variable and plot mean squared error over many possible slopes. They use the graph to locate a minimizer before connecting the idea to derivatives.

Ethics and equity prompt

A small average error can hide systematic errors for subgroups. Ask students to compare residuals by species or another group variable and discuss fairness in predictive performance.
